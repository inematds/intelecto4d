Frequently asked questions

Do I need to know how to code? +

No. We start from zero — including setting up the environment. Claude Code builds alongside you; your role is to decide what the assistant does.

What's the difference between online and in-person? +

The content is the same. In person, you get hands-on guidance by your side and networking with the group. Online, you participate live from wherever you are.

Do I need to pay for AI models? +

You’ll learn to use both models via OpenRouter (pay per use, just cents) and local models with Ollama (free, running on your machine).

Will I leave the workshop with something ready? +

Yes. The goal is to finish with your own assistant working on Telegram, with the features you chose.

Where can I see the full content? +

The detailed syllabus, track by track, is at inematds.github.io/intelecto-curso.
