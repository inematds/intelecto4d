Frequently asked questions

Do I need to know how to code? +

No. We start from zero, including setup. Claude Code builds alongside you; your role is to decide what the assistant does.

The content is the same. In person, you get hands-on guidance by your side and networking. Online, you participate live from wherever you are.

You’ll learn to use OpenRouter (pay per use, just cents) and local models with Ollama (free, on your machine).

Yes. The goal is to finish with your own assistant working on Telegram, with the features you chose.
